Scoring Methodology

Each engineer is scored 0–100 per bucket. Scores are normalized relative to the top performer in that metric across all 59 engineers analyzed. Primary bucket is whichever score is highest.

Feature Owner Score
merge_rate × 30% + dir_concentration × 25% + repeat_files × 25% + pr_volume × 20%
  • Merge rate: merged PRs ÷ total PRs opened
  • Dir concentration: % of PRs touching the same top-level directory
  • Repeat files: files touched 3+ times (normalized to top performer)
  • PR volume: total PRs authored (normalized)
Reviewer Score
review_volume × 25% + changes_requested_ratio × 30% + comment_substance × 25% + unique_authors × 20%
  • Review volume: total reviews given (normalized)
  • Changes requested ratio: CHANGES_REQUESTED ÷ all reviews (higher = more substantive)
  • Comment substance: ratio of reviews with >50 char body (normalized)
  • Unique authors: breadth — how many distinct engineers reviewed (normalized)
Infrastructure Score
infra_pr_ratio × 35% + infra_pr_volume × 35% + subsystem_breadth × 30%
  • Infra PR ratio: infra-touching PRs ÷ total PRs authored
  • Infra PR volume: raw count of infra PRs (normalized)
  • Subsystem breadth: unique infra areas touched: .github, ci, docker, k8s, scripts, bin, terraform, root-config (normalized)

Data source: PostHog/posthog — most recent 300 PRs targeting master within a 90-day window. Bots excluded. Minimum 3 PRs to qualify.
